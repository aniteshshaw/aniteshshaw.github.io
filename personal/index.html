02 · AI product · Creator · Private beta

QIS-Skills

A senior QA lead's checklist, written down for Copilot.

A library of GitHub Copilot agent skills for quality work: story reviews, test design, Playwright automation, PR checks, defect triage, RCA and accessibility. I rebuilt the playbooks behind QIS as generic skills that any team could pick up. I started it because good QA judgment shouldn't depend on which two or three people happen to be free. The catalog is private for now, and I demo it on request.

04 · Book · Author · In draft

AI-Native Quality Engineering

Quality is not a gate. It is a system.

A field guide for quality engineers and QE leads who've been asked to bring AI into their teams. It covers designing and testing AI skills, running them as a governed platform, measuring adoption honestly, self-healing automation that never hides a defect, RCA across an organization, and accessibility. The draft is finished and with reviewers. It isn't for sale.

The machine can perform the repeatable work. A quality engineer still decides what “good” means.
